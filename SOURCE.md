# Source

Based on [Velxio revision 94fd947](https://github.com/davidmonterocrespo24/velxio/tree/94fd947d6d85afddc7e66c927c7d494e7759f55b) and its `Dockerfile.standalone`. The Arduino target keeps the official frontend/Python/nginx design and copies Arduino CLI from the official image pinned in `upstream.lock.json`.

Velxio and this adapter use [GNU AGPL v3](LICENSE). The workspace's **Source & licenses** link serves `adapter/`, patches, dependency locks and build inputs; the exact upstream source is [available here](https://github.com/davidmonterocrespo24/velxio/archive/94fd947d6d85afddc7e66c927c7d494e7759f55b.tar.gz). Course templates, test solutions and student work are excluded from that archive. Dependency licenses remain with their respective projects.
